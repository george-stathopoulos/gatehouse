The problem

WordPress 7.0 gave every plugin your AI key

Connect Anthropic, OpenAI or Google once, and every plugin on your site can use it. That's convenient. It also creates three new risks.

One shared bill, no limit

WordPress has no spending cap. One buggy plugin or a spam wave can run up hundreds of dollars before your provider's invoice arrives.

No idea who spent it

Your provider bills one API key. It can't tell you whether the SEO plugin, the chatbot or the alt-text tool made the calls.

Customer data leaves your site

Emails, phone numbers and order details inside prompts go straight to a third-party AI provider, which matters under GDPR.

How it works

A gatehouse for your site's AI

Every AI request passes through Gatehouse on your own server. It checks, cleans and records it, in milliseconds.

  1. A plugin asks for AI

    For example, to write a product description through the WordPress AI Client.

  2. Gatehouse checks

    Identifies the plugin. Over budget or paused? The request stops here, before anything is charged.

    BUDGETS

  3. Gatehouse cleans

    Personal data becomes placeholders like [EMAIL_1]. Your brand brief is added.

    PRIVACY

  4. The provider answers

    Anthropic, OpenAI or Google only ever see the cleaned request.

  5. Gatehouse records

    Real values go back into the answer. The call is logged with plugin, model, tokens and cost.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which plugins does it work with?

Any plugin or theme that uses the AI Client built into WordPress 7.0 (wp_ai_client_prompt()). They need no changes. Plugins that call an AI provider directly with their own API key bypass the AI Client, so Gatehouse can't see them.

Does Gatehouse need its own API key or account?

No. It works with the provider you connect under Settings → Connectors, and never stores or sees your key itself.

Will it slow my site down?

Not noticeably. It does a budget lookup from a cached total, one pass over the prompt text and one database insert per call, all on your server.

What happens when a plugin reaches its budget?

Its next AI call is stopped before anything is sent, and the plugin receives the same error WordPress uses when AI is unavailable. Well-behaved plugins hide their AI features until the budget resets on the 1st of the month.

Are the costs exact?

They're close estimates: the token counts your provider reports, multiplied by current model prices. Your provider's invoice is always the source of truth. How costs are calculated.

Does Gatehouse send my data anywhere?

No. Its only optional outside request is a daily download of public model prices, which sends nothing about your site. Privacy details.

Is it accessible?

Yes. The dashboard is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: fully keyboard operable, a table view for every chart, never colour alone, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility details.
